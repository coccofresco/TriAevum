# TriAevum v0.6.0-alpha.3

## Sheik Can Finish a Sentence

More game, fewer arguments with the window. Still an unreasonable amount of grass.

- **Linux travels lighter.** One AppImage replaces Flatpak, with Forge included.
  Prepare in Desktop Mode, then add the AppImage to Steam for Gaming Mode.
  Steam Deck is a compatibility target, not a hardware-tested certification.
- **TopScreen gets its bearings.** Centered, proportion-preserving HUD, minimap
  and contextual-menu fixes, and Game Over choices that actually show up.
  The game may defeat you; the menu should not.
- **Sharper words.** TopScreen HD fonts are prepared by Forge and used
  automatically at 480p and above. No magnifying glass DLC required.
- **Controllers cooperate more.** Unified SDL routing, improved mouse/stick
  aiming and automatic motion aiming on supported controllers. Gyro affects
  aiming, not free camera. Virtual/third-party sensor support still varies.
- **Cutscenes can finish their business.** Sheikah Stone Visions playback and
  the Temple of Time revelation now progress through their previously blocked
  paths. The latter returns to gameplay on Windows and Linux.
- **Renderer housekeeping.** Improved frame pacing, grass visibility work and
  native shader infrastructure, plus the black-background/toon correction.
  This is not a promise of zero stutter or a universal FPS increase.

**Before updating:** back up saves and settings. Prefer normal in-game saves;
restoring a savestate made during the revelation's flashback remains unreliable.
F1 keeps the established settings menu. 60/90 FPS modes interpolate visuals;
game simulation remains 30 Hz. Android/macOS are not included in this release.

Windows: extract the portable ZIP and run `TriAevumForge.exe`. Linux: make the
AppImage executable and open it; use `--forge` to return to setup. Use your own
decrypted EUR/USA `.3ds` or `.cci` dump. Existing Flatpak data is not migrated
automatically: keep it backed up and separate from the new AppImage installation.
