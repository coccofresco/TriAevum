The first Moonfall Curse release: The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask 3D on the
TriAevum engine shared with Echoes of Time, with the mm3d-dekui interface and
Project Restoration built in.

## What it is

- Installed by its own Forge (built with the `title-mm3d` feature), which
  prepares Majora's Mask 3D only, from the user's own EUR CTR-P-AJRP dump. No
  ROM-derived data is distributed. No game assets are included in this package.
- Project Restoration's three layout archives are rebuilt by the Forge from the
  user's dump at install time; the package carries only their recipe, small
  BPS patches and Project Restoration's own members (GPL-2.0-or-later).
- The game executable is `Moonfall_Curse.exe` (Linux: `Moonfall_Curse`), with
  its launch profile `Moonfall_Curse.launch.json`; the Forge executable is
  `TriAevumForge(.exe)`, and the update helper carries the steel-mask icon.
- mm3d-dekui and Project Restoration are part of the product code, not Forge
  inputs.
- The engine's lighting, shadow and composition work is shared with Echoes of
  Time, with MM3D's title facts kept under `titles/mm3d`.

## Known limits

From the release review (G4a-F7) and the parity program:

- **dekui update slices not ported**: S4 (HD Gear photo, MenuBackdrop), S5
  (StickControls, OcarinaInput, EquipLabel) and InterpolationIdentity.
- The ported update-slice options are wired in with vanilla defaults; their
  live acceptance is still open. Translated prompts other than the file-select
  pair are proven by fixtures only.
- Owner-deferred (no run spent in this release): the inline I/II glyph look,
  the owl-statue native save round trip, a physical pad hot-plug and rumble
  session, and the savestate group (bow / first-person item, ObjMure room, talk
  target, pre-door Woodfall room 0, converted-actor scenes, the z2_sea light
  shaft, live room crossing, the F13 shop/price route, the Woodfall
  product-mode realtime run).
- F11 read timing remains open.
- G4a-F4 guest-patch return-check violations are reported but non-fatal in
  alpha 1 (fatal from alpha 2).
- The savestate group above ships marked NOT VERIFIED LIVE.
- The courtyard crawl hole issue reported for Echoes of Time (#105) could not
  be reproduced; it stays a known issue of the shared engine.
- macOS and VR are out of scope for this release (resumed right after it).
- Android is deferred.
