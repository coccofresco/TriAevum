# TriAevum artwork

The game icon, the Forge icon and the Forge background image were supplied by
the project owner for TriAevum. They are compiled into `TriAevum`,
`TriAevumForge` and `triaevum-update-helper` and installed as the desktop and
launcher icons of the Linux and Android packages. The masters and the
generator of every derived size are in `tools/triaevum_release/branding/` of
the corresponding source.

The artwork shows motifs associated with Nintendo's *The Legend of Zelda*
series (the Triforce, the Master Sword, the Ocarina). This notice grants no
rights in Nintendo trademarks or other Nintendo material; see
`LICENSE_SCOPE.md`, "Excluded material".
